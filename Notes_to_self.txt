    The gradient is clacualte after te forward pass and nbs ensure the gradienst 

    The gradient is calculated after the forward pass, and nbs ensures the gradients are accumulated over multiple micro-batches so that each weight update uses an “effective” batch of N = nbs samples, smoothing out noisy fluctuations. This makes the step size more consistent with the original learning-rate tuning (since LR is scaled by b/nbs) and reduces update variance, speeding convergence. 
    However, because BatchNorm still normalizes on each small micro-batch, some instability in the activations can persist. In practice, nbs gives you the optimization benefits of a large batch without needing all those images in GPU memory at once.
